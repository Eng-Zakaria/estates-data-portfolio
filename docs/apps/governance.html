It is the denominator used for energy and carbon intensity. estates-planningspace register (synthetic dim_building.gia_m2)on_changespacesinternalNever compare GIA with NIA in the same sentence: NIA is always the smaller number. P19 warns that OSM footprints are not floor area at all.
