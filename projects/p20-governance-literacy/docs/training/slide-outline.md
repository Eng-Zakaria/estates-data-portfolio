# Slide outline

Outline only. The slides are built by the human who delivers the session, in whatever tool the institution uses. Keep to roughly one slide per minute of talk, and no more than six words on a slide.

## Slide 1 - Title

- Data literacy for estates
- 45 minutes, three exercises
- All data shown today is synthetic

## Slide 2 - Why we are here

- Estate decisions are argued with numbers
- Most arguments are about meaning, not about the estate
- Today's rule: quote the definition and the date with the number

## Slide 3 - The three questions

- Net internal area per staff FTE
- Booked hours per week
- Overdue inspections as at 31 March
- Exercise 1: what would you ask first?

## Slide 4 - What each number measures

- Frequency: how often a room is booked
- Occupancy: how full it is when booked
- Utilisation: the two multiplied
- A blank is not a zero

## Slide 5 - Exercise 2: three claims

- 55% booked, 21% full, so close rooms
- No overdue inspections, so we are compliant
- Carbon fell, so we are on track
- Which are fair?

## Slide 6 - Filters and totals

- Filters change every number on the page
- Never add totals from two filters
- Check the as-of date before you quote anything

## Slide 7 - How good is the data

- Seven kinds of check, three severities
- Score measures the checks we wrote
- Quarantine means held back with a reason, not lost
- Exercise 3: the meter files score 82, what now?

## Slide 8 - Definitions and owners

- Every published metric has an owner
- Only the owner changes a definition
- Changes are logged with reason and impact
- Anyone can ask, through the data request process

## Slide 9 - Handling and classification

- Public, internal, confidential
- Estates analytics uses assets, spaces and counts, not people
- Personal data is not in these reports, on purpose

## Slide 10 - Where to look

- How to read our dashboards: one page per report
- Business glossary: what the words mean
- Metric registry: what the numbers mean
- Runbooks: what to do when something breaks

## Slide 11 - The habit

- Definition
- Date
- Owner
- Three questions, one minute

## Slides to avoid

- Screenshots of dashboards without a question attached. Use the live reports.
- Slides of standards documents. Say it in words and link the register.
- Data architecture diagrams. Not this audience, not this session.
