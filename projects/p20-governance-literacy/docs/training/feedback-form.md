# Feedback form

Two minutes at the end of the workshop. Print it, or paste it into a form. Anonymous is better: people are honest about the part that did not work.

```
DATA LITERACY FOR ESTATES - WORKSHOP FEEDBACK

1. Which part was most useful?
   [ ] The three questions a number must answer
   [ ] Reading a dashboard without being misled
   [ ] How good is the data behind the number
   [ ] Definitions, owners and change
   [ ] Something else: ______________

2. Which part was least useful, and why?
   _________________________________________________

3. Before today, how confident were you quoting a number from a
   dashboard in a meeting?
   [ ] not at all   [ ] a little   [ ] fairly   [ ] very

4. After today?
   [ ] not at all   [ ] a little   [ ] fairly   [ ] very

5. Name one thing you will do differently next time you quote a
   number.  ___________________________________________

6. Was anything confusing, missing, or too technical?
   _________________________________________________

7. Which report should the next session cover?
   [ ] Space utilisation   [ ] Assets and maintenance
   [ ] Energy and carbon   [ ] Compliance
   [ ] The data quality scorecard

8. Anything else?
   _________________________________________________
```

## How to use the answers

- Question 1 tells you what to repeat. Question 2 tells you what to cut.
- The gap between questions 3 and 4 is the honest measure of the session. If it is small, the workshop did not land and the issue is usually that people had no live dashboard to look at.
- Question 5 is the behaviour change you wanted. Follow it up two weeks later with one question: did it happen?
- Question 6 is free text and usually the most useful part. Fix what it names.

Keep the forms for one year so you can show whether confidence actually moved over several cohorts.
