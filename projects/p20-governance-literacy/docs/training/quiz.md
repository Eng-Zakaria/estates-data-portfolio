# Quiz: data literacy for estates

Ten questions. Five minutes. Answer in pairs. Answers are in the [answer key](answer-key.md).

## Questions

1. A report shows "utilisation 55%". What does that number combine?

2. A room has no bookings in the period. On a chart of utilisation, is that zero, or blank, and what does the difference mean?

3. You filter a dashboard to two buildings and add the two totals. What have you just produced?

4. Which is the safer statement to put in a report: "overdue inspections: 15", or "overdue inspections as at 31 March: 15"? Why?

5. Net internal area and gross internal area: which is larger, and which should you use for energy per square metre?

6. The data quality score for a dataset is 99 out of 100. What does that not tell you?

7. A safety inspection has no record at all. On a compliance dashboard, how does it appear, and why is that risky?

8. Scope 2 emissions here are location-based. What does that mean for a building with a green tariff?

9. Who may change the definition of a published metric?

10. You think a published number is wrong. What are the first two things to do?

## Extra challenge (for the confident)

11. A metric's caveat says "excludes uncovered events from the denominator". Why does the number move when the estate adds sensors?

12. Two dashboards use "space per person", one on net internal area and one on whole-building area. Both are quoted in the same committee paper. What is the problem?
